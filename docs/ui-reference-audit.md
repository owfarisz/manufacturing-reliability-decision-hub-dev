## Resulting design direction

The cockpit will use an 80 px icon rail, persistent asset/context bar, compact exception queue, a representative equipment illustration, one dominant trend and a decision rail. An asset overview answers identity, abnormality, suspected mechanism, supporting or missing evidence, and next human decision within the first 1440 x 900 viewport. Evidence, Decision, Action and Verification tabs progressively reveal detail. The chart and hotspot states will follow the selected recorded week; a simulated scenario remains visibly labelled. Source and approval rules remain reachable without turning the first screen into a long form.

The original 11 workbooks and six decks remain read-only. The April KO and May HE hourly signals are not synchronized with date-only weekly condition samples. KO hourly vibration velocity in MM/S is never compared with weekly displacement in micron. HE generic hourly discharge pressure is never renamed tube-side differential pressure. Any unavailable normalized value remains unavailable. All equipment art is an original explanatory drawing, not plant geometry.
